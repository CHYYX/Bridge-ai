Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Bridge processes information necessary to provide and operate its AI inference services. Customer prompts and model outputs are processed for the purpose of providing requested inference.

Bridge does not use customer prompts or model outputs to train AI models. Our service is designed not to intentionally retain raw prompt or output content after processing except where retention is required for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or where otherwise disclosed to the customer.

We may retain operational information such as request identifiers, timestamps, selected model, token usage, latency, service status, errors and billing-related metadata for service operation, monitoring, security and accounting.

Infrastructure may use temporary in-memory processing and caching mechanisms to provide inference and improve performance.

Customers should not submit information that they are not authorized to process through the service.
